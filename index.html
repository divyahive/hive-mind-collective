Hive Mind Collective is a community of women entrepreneurs in Santa Cruz who grow together, learn from one another's journeys, and access the resources that strengthen both their businesses and their well-being.

Learn more
Our Mission

"Our mission is to empower women entrepreneurs in Santa Cruz by creating a collective where members can grow together, learn from one another's journeys, and access resources that strengthen their businesses and their well-being."

How We Show Up

Five things this collective is built on.

Connection

Real relationships between women who understand the entrepreneurial road.

Collective Problem Solving

Bringing shared challenges to a room built to work through them.

Collaboration

Co-creating opportunities instead of building alone.

Learning & Insight Exchange

Trading hard-won knowledge across very different businesses.

Support & Encouragement

Celebrating wins and navigating challenges with honesty and care.
